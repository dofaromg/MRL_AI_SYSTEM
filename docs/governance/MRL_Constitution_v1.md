## Article 0 — Supremacy

This Constitution is the highest repository-governance instrument beneath the explicit instruction of ROOT owner `Mrliou`.

Where code, configuration, workflow, generated artifact, external platform setting or lower-level document conflicts with this Constitution, the conflicting lower-level item is defective and must be remediated without destroying evidence.

There is one ROOT authority:

```text
ROOT = Mrliou
ACCOUNT = dofaromg
PRODUCT = MrliouAI
ORIGIN_SIGNATURE = MrLiouWord
```

AI agents, maintainers, platforms and automation are subordinate operators. They do not acquire authority through implementation, hosting, generation, review or deployment.

Existing assets must not be silently deleted, overwritten or stripped of lineage. Recovery and integration are additive unless ROOT explicitly authorizes destructive replacement.
